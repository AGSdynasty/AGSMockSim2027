Practice the real thing. 12 teams, snake draft, your league's actual 2027 pick ownership, and landing spots from real NFL mock drafts.

How it works

Draft order. Projected order is the reverse of the current 2026 standings, with every traded 2027 pick sent to its real owner. It will change when the season ends. Random shuffles the order instead.

Landing spots. Each prospect is placed on an NFL team using five real mock drafts (Tankathon, ESPN, SI, Walter Football, Fansided). Consensus uses the most common team. Random mock picks one of the mocks for each player. Players who appear in no mock show TBD.

Values. Dynasty values are my own estimates on KeepTradeCut's superflex scale, since KTC hasn't posted its 2027 class. They blend published 2027 rookie rankings, expected draft capital and position. Treat them as opinions.

Other managers pick using those values, adjusted for what their roster needs and some randomness, so no two drafts play out the same.
